The Origin

Our Story

Founded by Kainat Abdullah, Luvo Organics began with a simple idea: everyday skincare should feel considered. We returned to traditional soap-making methods and started building small batches around botanical ingredients.

Each bar is shaped and finished by hand. From a carved leaf to a coffee-ground round, the forms are inspired by the ingredients themselves — made to bring a quieter, more personal touch to an everyday ritual.
